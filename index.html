Approach

Good product design reduces uncertainty.

01

Make system behaviour understandable

People should always know what is happening, what needs attention and what happens if they act.

02

Design around decisions, not data

A dashboard with hundreds of data points is a visualisation. An experience helps someone take the right action with confidence.

03

Build the conditions for good design

Clarity, trust, constructive critique and close work with product and engineering — so good decisions happen consistently.

Leadership perspective

Building a team is not the same as building a design culture.

Culture is shaped by how decisions are made: whether designers can challenge assumptions, join early enough to influence direction, and own outcomes rather than deliverables.
