01 — Overview

From a group of practitioners to a design function

At Nathan Digital in Dubai I led a distributed team of 19 designers and front-end developers. The work went beyond managing delivery: it meant establishing shared standards, supporting projects through to launch, managing stakeholder expectations and developing a culture where good design decisions could happen consistently.

06 — Team vs. culture

Building a team is not the same as building a design culture

A team can have talented designers, a strong design system and good tools — and still struggle to influence the product. Culture is shaped by how decisions are made.

01Can designers challenge assumptions?
02Are they involved early enough to influence direction?
03Do reviews encourage learning, or simply approval?
04Does the team understand the business problem behind the work?
05Are designers accountable for outcomes, not just deliverables?
